## 1. The 60-sec demo video

### Take protocol

- **Max 3 takes.** Best take by 2:10 PM.
- After each take: do NOT re-watch. Just glance at the runtime. If 56–62s, it's a take. If outside that range, redo.
- Edit in post only if a SPECIFIC mistake (cough, misspoke, wrong tab) is undeniable. Don't chase perfect.

### Verbatim script

> See [docs/demo-script.md](./demo-script.md) §"Script — verbatim".

**Key reminders for delivery:**
- Open flat and direct. No smile in the first 5 seconds. The bombed-pitches line lands harder if you're not selling.
- The screen-size toggle flip (🖥 → 📱) on Save Me at ~0:21 is the "fold-and-pocket" beat — slow down for 1 second on the phone view.
- The Refine moment on Coach Me at ~0:30 — type the Sam Altman note OUT LOUD as you type it ("Add Sam Altman"). Hit Refine. Pause. Show the new version.
- The Ship Me beat at 0:34–0:51 is THE moment. Speak more slowly. The line that wins social: **"CharisMaster just wrote its own launch page. The page I'm submitting to this hackathon — was generated by the submission."**
- The CTA "Coach you. Save you. Ship you." — punch each one.

### If live gen stalls during a take

1. Don't stop. Voiceover bridge: *"…here's what it generated for me earlier today."*
2. Cut tab to the pre-cached result tab (tab 5/6/7 from §0.1).
3. Continue the demo as if nothing happened.
4. In post, the cut is invisible.
