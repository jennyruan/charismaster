## The problem

Performers can't watch themselves perform. Founders bomb pitches they would have nailed if they'd seen the tape the day before. Comics tank sets that any working comic could have fixed with one note. Public speakers ramble through keynotes that a senior coach would have cut in half.

Coaching that solves this exists. It costs $300–$500/hour. It's gated to the top 1% who can afford it. Everyone else gets YouTube clips and crossed fingers.

## What CharisMaster does

CharisMaster is **one engine, three artifacts** — depending on whether you're trying to master the form, survive tomorrow, or launch a thing you built.

| Mode | When you use it | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| 🎓 **Coach Me** | "I want to get better at giving pitches over time." | A one-page how-to: 3 things the masters do, 3 traps to avoid, 3 drills you can run today, what to watch next. Reusable. |
| 🎯 **Save Me** | "I'm pitching YC in 12 hours." | A one-page personalized script: opening line, beats with timing marks, close + ask, prepared comebacks. Disposable. Fold and pocket. |
| 🚀 **Ship Me** | "I built a thing. Help me launch it." | A one-page launch page: product name, tagline, features, links, use guidance. Drop in design docs, your AI build chat history, a repo URL — CharisMaster synthesizes the launch page. (This very submission page was generated by Ship Me mode.) |

Same input flow: paste your performance text or a past video link, optionally describe the room. Same Gemini multimodal engine ingests your video + 3 canonical reference performances (YC Demo Day pitches for elevator pitch mode, TED talks for keynote mode, Mulaney/Birbiglia for stand-up). Different output template per mode. Both are screenshot-ready and printable.

**Both modes output graphical one-pagers**, not text reports. The user picks how to view it: 📱 phone for on-the-walk, 📲 tablet for backstage, 🖥 desktop for study sessions, 🖨 print for fold-and-pocket. One toggle switches between all four.

**Iterate without starting over.** Drop another reference video into the refine panel ("here's the YC pitch I love"), add a free-text note ("make the close more aggressive"), and CharisMaster regenerates the one-pager — keeping the parts you didn't critique. Version history persists across the session.

## Why Gemini (the technical wedge)

- **Native video understanding** — Gemini ingests your performance video AND 3 full reference videos in a single multimodal call. OpenAI and Anthropic cannot do this today. This is the wedge.
- **Long context** — 1M+ tokens lets us pass the full video corpus and structured output schema in one shot. No vector DBs, no RAG bullshit, no retrieval drift.
- **YouTube URL ingestion** — Gemini reads YouTube URLs directly. Our reference corpus is just a list of public links; zero ingestion infrastructure.
- **Nano Banana for the visual flourish** — every one-pager gets a hand-illustrated header generated inline.

Yoodli (the leading public-speaking-coach startup, $13M Series A) runs on transcript + filler-word detection + eye-contact heuristics. They can't ship our stack until they rebuild on Gemini 3. We start on the new primitive.
