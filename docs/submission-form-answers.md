## Why Gemini (required — this is a Gemini hackathon)

CharisMaster cannot exist on any other model in market today.

- **Native video understanding.** Gemini ingests your performance video AND 3 full reference clips in a single multimodal call. OpenAI and Anthropic cannot do this today. This is the structural wedge.
- **Long context.** 1M+ tokens lets us pass the full video corpus + your design docs + your AI chat history + the JSON output schema in one shot. Zero RAG, zero chunking, zero retrieval drift.
- **YouTube URL ingestion.** The reference corpus is just public URLs; Gemini reads them directly. Zero pre-download, zero storage.
- **Nano Banana (image generation).** Every one-pager gets a hand-illustrated header generated inline — sketchnote aesthetic, mode-specific.
- **Multi-input flexibility.** Ship Me takes design docs, AI chat transcripts, GitHub repo URLs, and freeform notes in the same call. Only Gemini's input surface handles that mix.

Yoodli ($13M Series A, the leading public-speaking AI) runs on transcript + filler-word detection + eye-contact heuristics. They cannot ship CharisMaster's stack until they rebuild on Gemini 3. We start on the new primitive.
