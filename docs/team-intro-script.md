# Team intro — 2-minute video script

> Single-take. Jenny on camera the whole time. Direct address.
> The team intro is sold separately from the product demo — this is the
> "why this team can ship this" video the judges watch alongside the
> 60-sec demo.

**Total runtime:** 120 seconds, hard cap.
**Recording surface:** OBS or phone. 1080p. Mic check.
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 with center-safe action so it crops square for socials.
**Wardrobe / setting:** what Jenny is already wearing today at the hackathon.
Authentic > polished. Pick a clean wall behind her, ring light if available.

---

## Why we don't pre-write a "team" intro

CharisMaster has one founder. So this is a **founder intro**, not a team intro.
Solo is a strength here, not a weakness — we lean into it.

---

## Pre-roll checklist

- [ ] Phone timer at 120 seconds, visible off-camera.
- [ ] Script printed or on a second screen behind the camera (not behind the lens — eyes need to be on camera).
- [ ] Camera at eye level. Not below the chin.
- [ ] One deep breath before recording starts.

---

## Script — verbatim (target: ~300 spoken words)

### 0:00–0:15 (Open — name, role, what you're building) — 15s

> "Hi. I'm Jenny Ruan. Solo founder of CharisMaster — an AI apprentice for high-stakes communication. Three modes: coach you, save you tonight, ship the thing you built. I made this prototype today, solo, in three hours, at the GDG Stanford × Red Bull Basement hackathon."

**Tone:** calm, direct, no smile yet. Establish credibility before warmth.

### 0:15–0:45 (Why this problem — the lived experience) — 30s

> "I've spent the last two years living this problem from two angles.
>
> Angle one: I did seventy stand-up shows in 2024. Seventy times I stood in front of a room with five minutes of material and no idea whether the punchlines were going to land until the moment they did or didn't. Every comic I know would tell you the same thing — you cannot watch yourself perform.
>
> Angle two: I'm a solo founder. I've pitched my last startup, YBuffet, to investors who I would not have bombed in front of if I'd seen the tape from yesterday's meeting. The good news is the feedback loop in performance is fixable. The bad news is it costs five hundred dollars an hour, and only the top one percent of performers can afford it."

**Tone:** warmer now. Tell the comedy story like a comic — pause after "land" and "didn't".

### 0:45–1:05 (Why I'm the one to build this) — 20s

> "I'm a strange profile for this. Mechanical engineering master's, three issued patents, three and a half years as a technical program manager. I shipped my first full-stack code six months ago. I also did seventy stand-up shows in the same window I taught myself TypeScript. The point isn't that I'm a unicorn. The point is — I'm the user. I am the founder who needs CharisMaster, and I'm the comic who needs CharisMaster. Founder-product fit is not a story I'm telling you. It's the reason this prototype exists."

**Tone:** confident, even-keeled. Don't apologize for the engineering background.

### 1:05–1:35 (What I've already shipped) — 30s

> "Two weeks before this hackathon, I shipped Vhostwriter — a voice-first ghostwriting agent on Cactus and Gemma 4. Solo. End to end. At a different YC hackathon. That product is live, the demo video has thousands of views, and I learned the exact stack I'd build CharisMaster on. Today I'm pulling the same trick: Gemini multimodal as the new primitive, AI Studio Build for scaffolding, Cloud Run for deploy, and a tight five-axis judging strategy. I don't need a co-founder to ship this prototype. I need three pilots."

**Tone:** matter-of-fact. Show, don't tell, that you ship.

### 1:35–1:55 (The ask + the close) — 20s

> "If you're an accelerator partner watching this, I want a pilot. YC, Techstars, a16z START — any of you. CharisMaster replaces the four-hundred-dollar-an-hour pitch coach your cohort can't all afford. If you're an L&D buyer, I want a pilot. Same engine, your top performers as the reference corpus.
>
> I'm here today, I'm here tomorrow, my email is on the one-pager. Let's get a coffee.
>
> Thank you."

**End frame:** hold on Jenny for 2 seconds in silence. Stillness is confidence. Cut.

## What the script deliberately does NOT do

- **Does not** mention ADHD as a positioning angle. (Real for Jenny; not a public differentiator.)
- **Does not** cite the discontinued Georgia Tech MSCS. (Not an active credential.)
- **Does not** frame Jenny as "non-technical." (She isn't.)
- **Does not** lead with the patents or the engineering CV alone. (Credibility, not headline.)
- **Does not** use CEO title. (Visa-protective per Jenny's O-1B status — Founder, President, or CTO are the safe titles.)
- **Does not** complain about the co-founder departure. (Solo is the strength, not the wound.)
- **Does not** mention specific dollar VC numbers as targets. (Hackathon team-intro is not a fundraise pitch deck.)

---

## Captions / open captions

Burn open captions onto the whole video — most social plays muted. High-contrast
white-on-black-shadow, bottom third. Captions are verbatim from the script.

---

## What to do if a take goes long

- Cut and restart from the last clean section break. Don't try to fix mid-sentence.
- Total recording time budget: 8 minutes (4 attempts max). After that, ship the best take.
- Acceptable runtime: 1:55 to 2:00. Under 1:50 = too rushed. Over 2:00 = trim either the Vhostwriter beat or one comedy sentence.

## Why this intro wins the 5 judging axes

| Axis | How this intro scores |
|---|---|
| Technical feasibility | Cited shipped product (Vhostwriter) + same stack used here. Proof, not promise. |
| Innovation & novelty | The "two angles" framing (comedy + founder pitching) is a genuinely rare biographical intersection. |
| Real-world applicability | Founder-product fit is the headline. Jenny IS the user. |
| Market potential & fundability | Closes with concrete asks (accelerator pilot, L&D pilot) — judges hear a GTM in 20 seconds. |
| **GTM / social traction** | **Solo founder narrative is the most viral founder-content shape on Twitter right now. The seventy-stand-up-shows detail is the share moment.** |
